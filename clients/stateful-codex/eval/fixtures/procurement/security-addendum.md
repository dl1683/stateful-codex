# Executed security addendum

Effective date: 2026-09-12

Section 4.2 supersedes inconsistent proposal language.

For Alder, a severity-one incident activates break-glass support in Virginia,
United States. The US support engineer may temporarily decrypt affected payloads
and view plaintext records. The data returns to the Frankfurt region after the
incident, but the parties agree that the temporary US access is processing
outside the EEA.

Birch and Cedar prohibit plaintext access by support personnel outside the EEA.
Their encrypted operational telemetry contains no customer-managed payload and
cannot be decrypted by vendor personnel.
