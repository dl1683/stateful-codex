# Remote-clinic connectivity log

The four longest verified upstream outages in the last twelve months were 88,
91, 94, and 96 consecutive hours. Clinics continued creating records throughout
each outage. The 96-hour event occurred at North Ridge from 2026-02-03 through
2026-02-07.

The architecture board used the observed maximum, 96 hours, as the minimum
offline acceptance requirement. A system that rejects records after 72 hours
would have lost 24 hours of intake during the North Ridge event.
