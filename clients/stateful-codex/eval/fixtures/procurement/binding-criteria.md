# Binding deployment criteria

Decision date: 2026-09-30

The committee approved three binding gates. A vendor is viable only if it
passes all three.

1. Regulated personal data must remain in the European Economic Area. This
   covers storage, processing, debugging, emergency support, and any temporary
   plaintext access. Encrypted diagnostic metadata may leave the EEA only when
   the customer holds the decryption keys and no vendor employee can view the
   underlying personal data.
2. Remote clinics must continue accepting records through at least 96
   consecutive hours without upstream connectivity. Queued records must retain
   their original timestamps and ordering.
3. The authoritative year-one total must not exceed $300,000.

Launch timing and feature preferences are important but cannot waive these
gates. Marketing claims about an EU hosting region do not by themselves satisfy
the first gate.
