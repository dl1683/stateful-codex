# Master license agreement

Executed: 2026-01-10.

Section 3.1 grants a worldwide license to distribute the covered diagnostic
software. Section 4.2 sets the running royalty at eight percent of net sales.

Section 7.3 states that each party's aggregate liability arising from the
agreement will not exceed $2,000,000. Section 7.4 excludes intentional
misconduct from that limitation.

Section 9.1 permits termination for convenience on 30 days' written notice.
Section 12.2 allows the parties to amend the agreement through a later written
instrument signed by both parties.
