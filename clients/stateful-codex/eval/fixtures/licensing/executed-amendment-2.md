# Executed amendment 2

Executed by both parties: 2026-03-12.

Section 1 replaces master-license Section 9.1. Termination for convenience now
requires 60 days' written notice.

Section 2 states: notwithstanding Section 7.3 of the master license, the
$2,000,000 aggregate liability cap does not apply to a party's data-security or
confidentiality breach, or to its intellectual-property indemnity obligations.

Section 3 says this amendment supersedes every inconsistent term in the master
license, an earlier amendment, or either party's proposal.
