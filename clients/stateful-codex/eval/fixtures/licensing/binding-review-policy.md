# Binding licensing review policy

Decision date: 2026-10-15.

The investment committee may describe the license as ready to close only if all
four conditions are satisfied:

1. Economics use the latest executed royalty terms and the approved finance
   schedule, not proposal headlines.
2. Material uncapped or carved-out exposure is identified rather than silently
   treated as subject to the master agreement's general liability cap.
3. Every territory requiring regulatory consent is either approved or excluded
   from the current grant.
4. The final closing record confirms approval; preliminary board minutes are
   not a final authorization.

Authority runs from the newest executed amendment or side letter, to earlier
executed amendments, to the master agreement, and only then to proposals.
Approved schedules control their subject matter. A later executed instrument
supersedes inconsistent earlier language.
